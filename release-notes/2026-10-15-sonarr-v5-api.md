SeerrNG now detects Sonarr 5.0 beta and uses its v5 API, while keeping support for Sonarr v3 and v4. Sonarr 5 season, tag, and monitoring updates use focused endpoints that preserve its metadata settings; missing v5 routes fall back to v3.
